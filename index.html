THE PROFESSION

What is a System Administrator?

A System Administrator, often called a SysAdmin, is an IT professional responsible for setting up, maintaining, and securing an organization's computers, servers, user accounts, and networks.

The goal is to make sure technology is reliable, available, and safe so people can work without unnecessary interruptions.
